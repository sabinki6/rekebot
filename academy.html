Course Curriculum

Unlimited Image Generation Masterclass

A hands-on, project-based course covering the full pipeline — from your first image on Google Colab to publishing on Civitai and beyond. No GPU required. Learn at your own pace, build a creative portfolio, and unlock optional publishing workshops.

Core Modules

Module 1 — Google Colab Setup & Runtime

Navigate Colab notebooks, manage GPU runtimes, install dependencies, and mount Google Drive for persistent storage. Zero local hardware needed.

Module 2 — Stable Diffusion Fundamentals

txt2img & img2img workflows, samplers, schedulers, and understanding how diffusion models generate imagery step by step.

Module 3 — Prompt Engineering & Parameters

Mastering CFG scale, steps, seeds, negative prompts, aspect ratios, and advanced prompt syntax including attention weights and BREAK tokens.

Module 4 — LoRAs, Checkpoints & ControlNet

Find, download, and apply LoRAs and fine-tuned checkpoints from Civitai. Use ControlNet (Canny, Depth, OpenPose) for precision-guided generation.

Module 5 — Civitai Platform Mastery

Browse models, understand version metadata, read community reviews, generate directly in the Civitai Generator, and build a creator profile to showcase your work.

Module 6 — Building a Consistent Creative Style

Develop a recognisable visual identity across a series of images using style LoRAs, seed locking, and controlled prompt variation.

Optional Workshop Add-on

Workshop — Comic Book Writing, Publishing & Selling Optional

Structure a compelling story arc and design consistent AI-generated characters panel by panel. Write dialogue, lay out full comic pages, then publish and sell your issues on SafariComix — set up your storefront, prepare print-ready files, price your issues, and run a launch campaign across East African comic book distribution channels.
